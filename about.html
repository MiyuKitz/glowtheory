About

Skincare that's worth the hype

Glow Theory helps you find skincare that suits your skin, from viral K-beauty to the luxury classics, without wasting money on hype.

What we do

We create buying guides for the results people actually want: clearer skin, a healthy glow, and smarter shopping. Every guide matches products to skin types and concerns, so you can pick with confidence.

How we choose products

  • Ingredient research. We read full ingredient lists and check what each key ingredient is known to do.
  • Expert sources. We follow guidance from published dermatology sources, like the American Academy of Dermatology, and link them in every guide.
  • Real reviews. We look at patterns across verified customer reviews, not one-off opinions.
  • No paid rankings. Brands can't pay to be included or to rank higher.

How we make money

Some links on Glow Theory are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes which products we recommend. Read our affiliate disclosure.
